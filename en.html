FNA & AMF Compliance

We used to fill out FNAs by hand.
For hours. Those days are over.

We forgot the explanatory letter every other file. We chased signatures. We lost 45 minutes per file. So we automated everything — AMF compliance from A to Z, signatures in 30 seconds, data synced across every module. And now the FNA includes an automated drawdown plan — built from the same information you've already entered. No more typing the same data 40 times.

How the FNA is built

One entry.
Ten guided steps.

Each step feeds the next: a piece of data entered once flows into the needs analysis, the budget, tax at death and the opportunities. At the end, all that's left is to sign.

👤
Contact & family
Identity, date of birth, sex, smoker status, marital status and dependent children. The spouse has their own record, with their own contracts.
Step 1 · Client record
🏠
Address & commitments
Google Maps address lookup, then mortgage, rent, auto and home insurance. Renewal dates feed the future opportunities.
Step 2 · Google Maps
💼
Employment & income
Type of employment, planned retirement age, and gross salary automatically converted to net income for every calculation that follows.
Step 3 · Gross → net
💰
Assets & liabilities
Current assets, liabilities, owner and marginal rate. You also plan the accounts to open, with a target date and the account's purpose.
Step 4 · Balance sheet
🛡️
Insurance in force
Every policy with the insured, the carrier and the amount. A policy can be excluded from the needs calculation with a single click.
Step 5 · Policies
🎯
Goals & opportunities
The client's goals become opportunities with a title, a target date and a next action. Nothing gets lost.
Step 6 · Next action
📈
Retirement
Asset projection, retirement income and tax impact, based on inflation, salary growth, life expectancy and RRSP contribution room.
Step 7 · Projection
🎓
Savings projects
Each project with its owner, account, target amount, monthly savings, initial amount and rate of return.
Step 8 · Quantified goals
🧮
Insurance needs
Life (income to replace, annuities for the spouse and children, funeral costs, inheritance), disability (income to protect, loan insurance) and critical illness in three plans.
Step 9 · Life · Disability · CI
⚖️
Tax at death
Capital gains and tax at death calculated from the data already entered, then built straight into the life insurance needs.
Step 10 · Built into needs
🏢
Corporate FNA
Profile and company, key people, finances, shareholders and agreement, goals, insurance, group benefits, trust, solo disability and analyses.

Automated AMF compliance

The replacement notice? 30 seconds. Done.

We were tired of filling out the same fields by hand on every file. Now the notice is filled in automatically — names, policies, amounts, reasons for replacement. The meeting transcript is built in, and the whole package goes out for e-signature in seconds.

The explanatory letter — the document that everyone forgets but that the AMF expects in every file — is generated and sent for signature automatically. Never a non-compliant file again.

And once it's signed? The notice is sent automatically to both insurers — the old one and the new one. No more hunting for "what's Beneva's compliance email again?" FINOX knows every address for every carrier. You sign, we handle the rest.

What used to take 45 minutes per file now takes a few clicks. You focus on the client. The paperwork is handled.
